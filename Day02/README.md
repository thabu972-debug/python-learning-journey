# 📘 Day 2 - Python Operators

Welcome to **Day 2** of my Python learning journey! 🚀

Today I practiced different types of **Python Operators**, which are essential for performing calculations, making comparisons, and controlling program logic.

---

## 📚 Topics Covered

### ➕ Arithmetic Operators
- Addition (`+`)
- Subtraction (`-`)
- Multiplication (`*`)
- Division (`/`)
- Floor Division (`//`)
- Modulus (`%`)
- Exponent (`**`)

### 🔍 Comparison Operators
- Equal to (`==`)
- Not Equal to (`!=`)
- Greater Than (`>`)
- Less Than (`<`)
- Greater Than or Equal To (`>=`)
- Less Than or Equal To (`<=`)

### 🧠 Logical Operators
- AND (`and`)
- OR (`or`)
- NOT (`not`)
